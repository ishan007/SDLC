Exactly-once submission trade-offs

Context: when an invoice is approved, it must be submitted to an external payment provider, PayEx, exactly once. Facts:

PayEx returns a reference on success. It times out at 30 seconds, and a timeout does not reliably mean the submission failed.
About 40,000 invoices a month, 60% in the last three working days, peaking near 80 approvals a minute.
Azure App Service, Azure SQL, and Azure Service Bus (provisioned, unused).
Approval actions must be auditable for seven years (SOC 2 Type II).
Today the app has no persistence and no PayEx integration (see backend/).

Compare these options:

1. An idempotency key with a dedupe table
2. A transactional outbox with a worker
3. Optimistic submission with nightly reconciliation

For each give: how it achieves exactly-once (or why it only achieves at-least-once or at-most-once), what it assumes about PayEx, failure modes, operational load on a six-person on-call team, and audit impact. 
Say which option, or combination, you recommend and why. 
State any assumption about PayEx you could not verify, for example whether it accepts an idempotency key or offers a lookup-by-reference endpoint, and how the recommendation changes if that assumption is false.

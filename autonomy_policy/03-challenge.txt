Challenge policy

Autonomy levels used in the policy:

L0  Off: the agent does not do this task.
L1  Suggest: the agent reads and proposes; a human does the action.
L2  Draft for approval: the agent prepares the action; a named human approves before it runs.
L3  Act with guardrails: the agent acts within fixed limits (read-only, reversible, rate-limited) and tells a human straight away; a human can undo it.
L4  Autonomous: the agent acts and reports afterwards; a human reviews a sample.

Find the weakest points:

Which task has a level its controls do not justify?
Which "evidence to move up a level" could be satisfied without the task actually being safe?
Is every move up a level approved by a named role, and is that role able to judge the evidence? Is anyone approving their own move?
Is the day-one level realistic for a six-person rotation where two engineers are new to the codebase? Consider who is on call alone at 3 a.m.
Does the policy fit on one page? If not, what is padding?

Do not rewrite the policy. List the issues with a one-line reason each.
